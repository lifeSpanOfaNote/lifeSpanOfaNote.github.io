This paper develops a diffusion model-based approach for learning to simulate from heavy-tailed distributions. The method is particularly useful for applications in finance and risk management where heavy-tailed distributions are prevalent.

**Authors**: Tingyu Zhu*, Haoyu Liu*, Nanshan Jia, Jinghai He, Zeyu Zheng (*co-first author)

*Operations Research*, 2026.
