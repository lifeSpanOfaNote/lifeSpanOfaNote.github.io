On open-ended design problems, frontier LLMs can identify flaws in their designs but cannot reliably fix them. This paper builds a stochastic framework linking LLM generation, requirement structure, and revision, and derives stop-and-restart rules for the LLM-based design generation and revision process.

**Authors**: Tingyu Zhu, Zeyu Zheng
