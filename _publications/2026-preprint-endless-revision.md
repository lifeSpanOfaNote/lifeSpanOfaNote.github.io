On open-ended decision problems, frontier LLMs can identify flaws in their decisions but cannot reliably fix them. This paper builds a stochastic framework linking LLM generation, requirement structure, and revision, and derives stop-and-restart rules for the LLM-based decision generation and revision process.

**Authors**: Tingyu Zhu, Zeyu Zheng
