Spiral RoPE extends the standard axial 2D Rotary Position Embedding in vision transformers beyond horizontal and vertical directions. Embedding channels are partitioned into groups associated with uniformly distributed directions, and each group is rotated according to the projection of the patch position onto its direction. Spiral RoPE consistently improves performance across classification, segmentation, and generation tasks.
